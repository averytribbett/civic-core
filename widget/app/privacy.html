Chat Privacy Notice

Last updated August 16, 2026

Scope

This notice describes how Civic Core LLC (“Civic Core,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use the Civic Core chat widget (the “Widget”) embedded on a city or county website.

The government organization that hosts the Widget on its site (the “Client”) may also have its own privacy policies. This notice explains Civic Core’s practices as the provider of the Widget.

Information we collect when you chat

  • Messages. Questions you submit and AI-generated replies shown in the chat.
  • Language preference. The language you select in the Widget interface.
  • Feedback. Optional thumbs up or down votes on responses.
  • Conversation identifier. A random ID used to group messages in a single chat session.
  • Technical data. Information our servers log for security and operation, such as IP address, browser type, request timing, and error logs. We use IP addresses for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

Information we do not intentionally collect

The Widget does not require an account, payment details, or a login. Please do not submit sensitive information (such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or health records) in the chat. We do not design the Widget to collect those categories of data.

How we use information

  • Generate answers using AI and content from the Client’s public website.
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Widget and our backend services.
  • Improve answer quality and reliability, including reviewing aggregated usage patterns.
  • Respond to support requests, legal obligations, or Client directions.

AI processing

Chat messages are processed by OpenAI to generate responses. Civic Core uses OpenAI with a zero data retention (ZDR) configuration for this processing. AI answers may be inaccurate or incomplete. Always verify important information with official government sources.

Service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers that help us deliver the Widget, including:

  • OpenAI — AI inference for chat responses.
  • Google Cloud — application hosting (Cloud Run).
  • Database hosting — secure storage of chat logs (PostgreSQL, e.g. Supabase).
  • Firebase Hosting — delivery of Widget files.

Open-source libraries used to render chat messages in your browser (for example, Markdown rendering and HTML sanitization) run locally on your device and are not listed as subprocessors. See our Subprocessor List for details.

We may also share information with the Client whose website embeds the Widget, consistent with our agreement with that Client.

Retention and security

We retain chat data as long as needed to provide the Widget, meet our Client agreements, and comply with law. When encryption is enabled in our environment, message content is encrypted at rest.

No system is perfectly secure. Use the Widget at your own discretion for non-sensitive questions about public services.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to use the Widget. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you.

To submit a privacy request, email avery@civiccore.ai. We may need to verify your request and coordinate with the Client that operates the host website.

We do not currently respond to Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals.

Children

The Widget is a general public-information tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
